# Crowbar Heist Kit

36 SFX and 4 music cues, Ogg Vorbis at 44.1 kHz. SFX are mono except sirens, alarm bell and car engine. Music is stereo. Longer cues target -16 LUFS integrated and true peak ≤ -1 dBTP; subsecond effects target -3 dBFS peak.

Loops carry LOOPSTART=0 and LOOPLENGTH in samples. Music loops have 3,969,000 samples (90 seconds). Sneak and alarm share an eight-bar source bed arranged over 48 bars at 128 BPM; start them together and crossfade their gains at the same playback offset. Getaway targets 104 BPM over 39 bars. Payout is a six-second one-shot with natural decay.

Music key and phrasing labels are generation targets, not certified musical analysis. Audition the paired cues before using synchronized transitions.
